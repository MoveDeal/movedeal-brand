Official product mark

The mark

A geometric MD monogram in Brand #5B4BFF with a cyan anchor #19D3FF. Filled paths (M + open D + arrow) mapped from the brand sheet. Shipped as SVG with outlined Inter Bold wordmark — no live type.

What it stands for

MoveDeal connects people who need to move with verified movers. The monogram is compact for app icons and favicons; the lockup carries the full MoveDeal name.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the mark equal to the stroke width (~11% of mark height) on all sides. Never crop the cyan accent dot or the diagonal uplift.

Usage

Do

  • Use color MD mark on Paper / white digital surfaces
  • Use white-mark / dark lockup on dark and Brand fields
  • Keep proportions locked — scale uniformly
  • Use the simplified favicon below 24px (chip letters will not hold)
  • Use Cyan only for accents, motion, and focus — not body text

Don’t

  • Don’t stretch, rotate, or add shadows / gradients to the mark
  • Don’t recolor the monogram outside approved variants
  • Don’t remove the AI chip or leaf from the primary mark
  • Don’t ship live HTML/SVG text for the wordmark — use the outlined files
  • Don’t place low-contrast blue on blue photography without a panel
