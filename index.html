Official product mark

The mark

A left-facing moving van in Ocean #0077B6. Cargo carries an AI chip (intelligent quotes) and a leaf (responsible moving). Geometry is traced from the live 100×100 product file and shipped as fill-based SVG — no live type, no strokes.

What it stands for

MoveDeal connects people who need to move with verified movers. The mark reads at a glance: logistics (truck), intelligence (AI), and care for the journey (leaf).

Clear space

Keep empty space around the mark equal to the cab height (~18% of the mark height) on all sides. Never crop wheels, the ground line, the chip, or the leaf.

Usage

Do

  • Use Ocean truck on Paper / white digital surfaces
  • Use reversed or on-ocean marks on dark and Ocean fields
  • Keep proportions locked — scale uniformly
  • Use the simplified favicon below 24px (chip letters will not hold)
  • Use Signal only for CTAs and sparse accents

Don’t

  • Don’t stretch, rotate, or add shadows / gradients to the mark
  • Don’t recolor the truck outside approved variants
  • Don’t remove the AI chip or leaf from the primary mark
  • Don’t ship live HTML/SVG text for the wordmark — use the outlined files
  • Don’t place low-contrast blue on blue photography without a panel
